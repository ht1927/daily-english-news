Source: CNBC — Intel Q1 2026 earnings report, Apr 23 2026

📚 Vocab

重要単語 重要表現
Intel posted strong first-quarter 2026 results that significantly exceeded Wall Street expectations, sending its shares surging nearly 20% in after-hours trading. The chipmaker reported adjusted earnings of 29 cents per share on $13.58 billion in revenue, blowing past consensus estimates of just 1 cent per share and $12.42 billion. Analysts credited the rebound to stronger demand for AI-related chips, aggressive cost-cutting, and the company's renewed focus on its core business. The results also eased concerns over whether Intel was falling behind in the AI race dominated by rivals like Nvidia and AMD. Meanwhile, fellow chipmaker Texas Instruments reported robust guidance, with shares jumping 19%. Together, these results are energizing the broader semiconductor sector. Investors now await next week's earnings from Microsoft, Amazon, Alphabet, and Meta.

Kohler has unveiled its latest smart-bathroom innovation at KBIS 2026: a health-sensing toilet system called Dekoda. Remarkably, it can even detect the presence of blood in the bowl, giving users an early warning of potential medical issues. Alongside Dekoda, Kohler introduced the Leap smart toilet and the PureWash E860 bidet seat, which feature a dual-wand design — an industry first aimed at improving women's hygiene. These products reflect the company's strategic pivot toward preventive health. Industry observers say such innovations are redefining the bathroom as a wellness hub rather than a simple utility space. Consumers increasingly expect daily fixtures to deliver value beyond basic function.
