South Korea's GDP Surges on AI Chip Demand

韓国GDP、AI向け半導体需要で急成長
Source: Rio Times — Global Economy Briefing, Apr 23 2026
📖 Read
📚 Vocab
💡 Expressions
重要単語 重要表現
South Korea's economy has achieved its fastest growth in over five years, driven by soaring semiconductor demand. According to quarterly data from the Bank of Korea, the country's GDP expanded significantly in the first quarter of 2026, outperforming market expectations. The surge was primarily fueled by global demand for advanced chips used in artificial intelligence systems and data centers. Major manufacturers such as Samsung Electronics and SK Hynix have reported record exports, boosting industrial output across the nation. Economists attribute this momentum to increased capital expenditure from tech giants worldwide investing in AI infrastructure. However, analysts caution that the growth remains heavily dependent on a single sector, which could pose risks if global chip demand slows. Despite concerns, the positive figures have lifted investor confidence and pushed the Korean won to a three-month high against the US dollar.

The Rise of "Social Saunas" and Sauna Tourism

「ソーシャルサウナ」とサウナツーリズムの興隆
Source: Global Wellness Summit — 10 Wellness Trends for 2026
📖 Read
📚 Vocab
💡 Expressions
重要単語 重要表現
Sauna culture is undergoing a major transformation as "social saunas" gain popularity around the world. Unlike traditional saunas focused on endurance and extreme heat, these new venues prioritize relaxation, emotional well-being, and community. According to industry analysts, the global sauna market is expected to exceed $1 billion by the mid-2020s, with the U.S. sector alone surpassing $200 million in annual revenue. Meanwhile, sauna tourism has emerged as a rapidly growing niche within the wellness travel industry, currently valued at over $4 billion globally. Europe accounts for nearly half of this market and is projected to grow by 6-7% annually through the next decade. Finland, Estonia, and Japan remain top destinations for enthusiasts seeking authentic experiences. Innovations such as infrared heating and smart sensors are also reshaping home saunas. Experts believe this ritual-based approach to wellness will continue to thrive as people prioritize mental reset over physical extremes.

IKEA's $10 Bluetooth Speaker Shakes Up the Audio Market

Source: Gear Patrol — CES 2026 Tech Releases
📖 Read
📚 Vocab
💡 Expressions
重要単語 重要表現
IKEA has unveiled a surprising new product at CES 2026: the Kallsup Bluetooth speaker, priced at just $10 per unit. Despite its incredibly low cost, the speaker offers a clever feature that sets it apart from competitors — the ability to daisy-chain multiple units together to form a wireless speaker array. Users can connect several Kallsup speakers wirelessly, enabling them to play synchronized audio throughout multiple rooms without investing in expensive smart home systems. This approach makes high-quality, multi-room sound accessible to budget-conscious consumers. The device features a compact, minimalist design that aligns with IKEA's signature Scandinavian aesthetic. While audio purists may point out its modest sound specifications, reviewers highlight its exceptional value proposition and creative connectivity. Industry observers note that IKEA's entry into affordable audio tech could disrupt the market and pressure established brands to lower prices.
